Merge precedence is chosen per field by each implementer's own Merge code, not by go-cfgstore. For each field, a project decides which rule applies: project-only (never inherited from a lower layer), global-only (taken from the lower, user-level layer alone), project-wins (the higher layer's non-empty value, else the lower's), or global-wins (the lower layer's non-empty value, else the higher's). go-cfgstore supplies the layers and their order and calls Merge; it imposes no rule of its own. Rejected: declarative rules supplied by go-cfgstore, such as struct tags or a rule table, which cannot express a rule that depends on more than one field or on the project's own needs.
