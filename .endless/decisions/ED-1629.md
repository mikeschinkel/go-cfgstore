Merge precedence is chosen per field by each implementer's own Merge code, not by go-cfgstore. The default is project-wins: the higher layer's non-empty value, else the lower's. A field departs from it only when the project needs one of the other rules: project-only (never inherited from a lower layer), global-only (taken from the lower, user-level layer alone), or global-wins (the lower layer's non-empty value, else the higher's). go-cfgstore supplies the layers and their order and calls Merge; it imposes no rule of its own. Declarative rules supplied by go-cfgstore, such as struct tags or a rule table, are deferred, not rejected: there are not yet enough use cases to identify a pattern that would cover every rule implementers need.
